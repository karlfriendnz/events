One-line summary. The enhanced Events + Bookings core is built and exceeds the old system, and so is the foundation layer — federation, roles, custom-field engine, terminology, and People CRM with contacts/circles. The major work still ahead is the Terms/Squads membership spine, the real Billing engine, the entire Competitions module, and the secondary modules + integrations/compliance layer.

3Read-out

What's solid. Roughly Phase 0 and a good chunk of Phase 1 are delivered. In Events and Bookings the rebuild is materially ahead of the legacy platform — multi-session events, a reusable form engine, three booking flows, and access control have no equivalent in the old system. The federation, roles, custom-field and contacts/circles foundations are also in place.

What's the critical path. Three workstreams gate "feature-parity with the old system":

  • Terms / Squads spine — the registration window + membership-over-time backbone the whole legacy app hangs off.
  • Billing engine — a genuine engine (pro-rata, subscriptions, Xero, direct debit), not just fee lines.
  • Competitions — ~30 tables of net-new, the largest single build.

Everything else (secondary modules, mobile API, integrations, compliance) is additive and module-toggleable once those three land.
